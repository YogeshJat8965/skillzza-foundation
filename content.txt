Why we exist
India's greatest asset is its youth. Most programmes meet them only once.
A girl gets a scholarship but drops out before college. A graduate finishes a course but never lands a job. A young woman has a business idea but no mentor, network or first rupee of capital. Each problem has a programme — but nobody stays with the young person from one step to the next.

Skillzza Foundation is built to change that. We stay with every young person across their whole journey — school, skills, career and enterprise — and we measure success by the lives that move forward, not the seats that get filled.
